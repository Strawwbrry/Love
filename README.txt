COUPLE MUSIC CARD — READY TO USE.

1. Put these four files in the same folder:
   - index.html
   - style.css
   - script.js
   - couple.jpg
   - song.mp3

2. Replace couple.jpg with your photo of you and your boyfriend.
   Keep the filename "couple.jpg", or change the filename in index.html.

3. Replace song.mp3 with your own audio file.
   Keep the filename "song.mp3", or change the filename in index.html.

4. Open index.html in Edge or Chrome.

5. Click "Click here to play our song".
   The song starts because the play action is triggered by your click.

To change the song title and artist, edit these lines in index.html:
   <h1 id="songTitle">Yellow</h1>
   <p id="artistName">Coldplay</p>

The design is inspired by the uploaded music-player reference, but the photo itself is NOT included.
